Arroz con Atun

A plate with rice, eggs, and tuna. I like to add a lil bit of mayonnaise

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Boiled Eggs
  • Atun
  • Salt, mayonnaise, whatever u want to put on it

Steps

  1. Put the eggs to boil
  2. Cook the rice a little bit with olive oil.
  3. After the rice gets transparent, put the double water that rice (half cup of rice, one cup of water)
  4. Open the tuna can, and after peel the eggs.
  5. When the rice is ready, mix all in the same pot
  6. Enjoy the food.
