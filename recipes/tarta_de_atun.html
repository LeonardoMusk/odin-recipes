Tarta de Atun

One of my favourites dishes. MY brother Pablo make it this dish really often.

Ingredients

  • tuna
  • Boiled Eggs
  • some veggies
  • Mass for the tarta

Steps

  1. Put the eggs to boil
  2. Cut the veggies and fry them together.
  3. Add the tuna. WHen the filler is ready, put to get some cold
  4. GEt the mass ready, and put all the filler inside.
  5. Take it to the oven.
  6. Eat the tarta.
